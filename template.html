How to read this. The web version of the Training Compliance Power BI dashboard. LMS is Paylocity LMS coursework (from the daily learner transcript); Safety101 is job-qualification sign-offs from app.safety101.com.

Needs attention lists anyone whose noncompliant items — LMS courses in the statuses picked by the LMS chip, plus Safety101 qualifications not fully signed off — exceed 20% of everything assigned to them across both systems. It follows the manager/location filter and the LMS chip. Safety101 items signed off at the partial level count as noncompliant here.

The LMS gauge is Completed ÷ (Completed + Overdue) — courses not yet started or in progress don’t count against it, exactly like the Power BI gauge. The Safety101 gauge credits partial sign-offs as half. The by-location chart uses the dashboard’s stricter column math (LMS: anything not overdue; Safety101: fully signed off only), so it won’t match the gauges exactly. Locations with zero Safety101 compliance are left off the chart, matching the original.

Gauges and the chart ignore the two status chips — those only change which rows the two tables and the needs-attention list show. Sources refresh on their own schedules: .
