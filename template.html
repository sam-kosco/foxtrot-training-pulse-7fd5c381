How to read this. The web version of the Training Compliance Power BI dashboard. LMS is Paylocity LMS coursework (from the daily learner transcript); Safety101 is job-qualification sign-offs from app.safety101.com. Every item is either Compliant or Overdue.

Every item is Completed, Overdue, or Incomplete — and Incomplete items don’t count either way: like walks in a batting average, they’re left out of the math entirely. Every percentage is Completed ÷ (Completed + Overdue).

On LMS, Incomplete means a course that’s not started or still in progress. On Safety101, anything not fully signed off (including partial sign-offs) is Overdue — unless the employee was hired within the last 10 days (per Paylocity hire dates), in which case their gaps are Incomplete while they get trained up.

Needs attention lists anyone with more than 20% of their counted LMS courses + Safety101 qualifications overdue. O / I / C columns show each person’s overdue / incomplete / completed counts per platform. It follows the manager/location filter, like everything else on the page.
